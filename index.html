Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cognix browser safe to use?

Absolutely. Cognix is built on the secure Chromium core, but stripped of all trackers and telemetry. We undergo regular independent security audits to ensure your browsing identity is always protected.

Is it entirely free?

Yes! Cognix Browser is 100% free to download and use without restrictions. We believe privacy and speed should be a right, not a paid privilege.

Does it block YouTube ads and pop-ups?

Yes. Our native ad-blocking engine is integrated at the network level. It seamlessly blocks YouTube video ads, annoying pop-ups, banners, and invisible tracking scripts across all sites instantly.

Does it actually use less RAM?

Yes. We use an aggressive tab-sleeping algorithm and stripped-down rendering processes. Cognix unloads background tabs from memory automatically and blocks heavy ad-scripts, saving up to 70% of RAM compared to standard browsers.
